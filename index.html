How It Works

Connect once. Know your risk forever.

Set up once, then let the workflow do the reconciling — today from uploaded data, with live connectivity expanding next.

1

Bring Your Data In

Upload invoices and receipts (OCR-extracted automatically) and your bank statement as a CSV. Live bank connectivity via India's Account Aggregator framework is in active development.

2

See Your Risk, Ranked

Every transaction, every invoice, every vendor's GST filing status — matched and cross-checked, then scored by rupee impact and urgency. Not a spreadsheet of maybes. A ranked list of what actually matters.

3

Investigate Before It's Too Late

Get surfaced the moment ITC looks at risk or a mismatch appears — while the filing window is still open and there's still time to act. Not buried in a report. Now.

Trust & Transparency

We'd rather be precise than impressive.

MarginPulse is in controlled beta. The reconciliation workflow above is real and in active commercial validation — we're not going to dress it up with numbers we can't back or names we can't confirm. As real usage grows, this section grows with it.

MarginPulse helps finance and tax professionals identify potential issues. It does not replace a CA, tax professional, or auditor, and does not make filing or compliance decisions on your behalf.
